ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

Nurturing Roller Skating Brilliance in Kanchipuram

The Kanchipuram District Roller Skating Association (KDRSA) is a recognized district unit under the Tamil Nadu Roller Skating Association (TNRSA), which is proudly affiliated with the national governing body, the Roller Skating Federation of India (RSFI).

Grassroots Focus

Identifying and preparing raw district-level talent from the tender age of 4+.

Pathway To Glory

Official qualifiers leading straight to TNRSA State & RSFI National Arenas.

Annual Events

Hosting premium, district level events featuring speed and artistic skating.

Strong Culture

Fostering top tier discipline, active physical fitness, and strong character.

Our Vision

To establish Kanchipuram as one of India's strongest and most revered roller skating hubs. We envision a sustainable sports structure capable of producing elite physical powerhouses, world champions, and Olympic-bound speed skaters representing the tri-color on the global podium.

Our Mission

To systematically deliver highly professional coaching pathways, construct modernized training arenas, facilitate robust, ethically structured district competitions, and foster strong parent-coach collaboration. We guide dedicated athletes seamlessly from beginners to international champions.
